Consultation & Study Design

02

Molecular Modeling & Cheminformatics

Prepare chemical inputs and explore molecular properties with documented assumptions.

Scope and methods

Small-molecule structure assessment, stereochemistry, conformations, descriptor calculations, structure comparisons, and receptor/ligand preparation. Tools include RDKit, PDBFixer and compatible parameterization workflows.

Typical deliverables

Prepared SDF/PDB structures, descriptor tables, input-validation summaries.

Discuss this service →
03

Molecular Docking & Virtual Screening

Investigate possible binding modes and prioritize hypotheses for further study.

Scope and methods

Structure-based protein–ligand docking, receptor and ligand preparation, docking-region design, pose comparison, selected virtual screening and qualitative interaction interpretation. Tools include AutoDock Vina and Meeko.

Typical deliverables

Predicted poses, score tables, prepared coordinates, molecular interaction views, limitations.

Discuss this service →
04

Molecular Dynamics

Examine molecular behavior over time using documented simulation protocols.

Scope and methods

Supported biomolecular system setup, equilibrium protocols, independent MD replicas, production simulations, checkpointing, and method-specific QC. OpenMM and GROMACS are within the service scope, with methods selected per project.

Typical deliverables

Simulation inputs, logs, agreed trajectories, run provenance, QC summaries and selected checkpoints.

Discuss this service →
05

Trajectory Analysis & Interpretation

Turn simulation trajectories into quantitative structural evidence.

Scope and methods

RMSD, RMSF, radius of gyration, hydrogen-bond and contact analyses, conformational comparisons, stability metrics, and appropriate replicated-study summaries using Python, MDTraj and MDAnalysis.

Typical deliverables

Analysis tables, methodological definitions, plots, figures, and written technical interpretation.

Discuss this service →
06

Visualization & Scientific Reporting

Communicate methods and results clearly, with evidence suitable for scientific review.

Scope and methods

Molecular illustrations, docking pose figures, trajectory animations, reproducibility records, methods documentation, figures and scoped technical reports using PyMOL and Python-based workflows.

Typical deliverables

Publication-resolution graphics, labeled figures, available supporting data, technical reports and animation files when scoped.

Discuss this service →

STANDARD DELIVERY

Research outputs that support review

Depending on scope, projects include a methods record, documented inputs, agreed numerical results, relevant figures, quality-control findings, and a concise scientific interpretation.

Scientific limitations matter. Docking scores are predictions, not measured binding affinities. Simulation results do not guarantee biological activity, experimental success, or clinical efficacy.
