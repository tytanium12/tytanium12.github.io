BILLING POLICY

Clear scope. Clear costs. No unexpected overruns.

Each approved engagement identifies labor, any computing charges, deliverables, and a spending ceiling.

Third-party GPU providers

Documented external rental charges pass through at actual cost, without markup.

Local computing

Charged at a disclosed, agreed cost-recovery rate (initial planning estimate: $0.26/GPU-hour). Hardware suitability and actual usage are recorded.

Data and storage

Standard delivery and limited temporary retention within the agreed scope are included; exceptional storage or transfer is quoted.

Scope changes

Additional work and expenses require approval before exceeding the agreed spending limit. Avoidable technical rework is not automatically billed to the client.
