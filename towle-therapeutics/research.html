FEATURED PUBLICATION

A published molecular docking study

Our 2018 fluoroquinolone study combined docking-based interaction hypotheses with synthesis and experimental testing. The original work used the SYBYL-X docking suite.

Representative docking explored whether extended N-1 substituents could approach the active-site tyrosine in a topoisomerase–DNA complex. The published figure shows six predicted poses of a quinolone derivative.
