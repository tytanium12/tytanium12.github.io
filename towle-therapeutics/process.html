THE CLIENT EXPERIENCE

From first inquiry to a documented scientific handoff.

A clear process for project planning, controlled costs, scientifically appropriate methods, and useful results.

01

Send an inquiry

Share a general, nonconfidential research objective through our questionnaire or email. We assess whether your request is a potential fit; inquiry is free.

02

Initial scientific consultation — $100

One hour of professional time: approximately 15 minutes preliminary review and 45 minutes discussion. Receive a brief recap of potential approaches and next steps.

03

Review the written scope

Receive a basic commercial estimate at no extra charge, with assumptions, labor, required computing, deliverables, a spending ceiling, and expected milestones. Detailed protocol development is paid after approval.

04

Approve the engagement

Agree on the scientific methods, data-sharing restrictions, authorized hardware/cloud providers, intellectual-property terms, fees, and payment arrangements before substantial work begins.

05

Execution and quality control

We prepare systems, run approved computational work, document environments and parameters, track issues, and perform agreed method-specific validation and analysis.

06

Receive results and interpretation

Receive the agreed scientific outputs and a technical summary, including relevant methods and limitations. Additional follow-up work is available under a new or extended scope.

PROJECT HANDOFF

What arrives at the end

Project outputs vary with the approved scope, but we aim for organized, inspectable research deliverables.

▤
Methods and protocol

Software identity, settings, scientific assumptions and relevant limitations.

▤
Results and data

Agreed structures, trajectories, numerical tables, or reports.

▤
Figures and analysis

Visualizations, plots, and interpretable technical summaries.

▤
Reproducibility record

Relevant hashes, configuration, provenance, and validation documentation.
