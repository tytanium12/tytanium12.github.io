TowleVision Policies

Practical production service policies

A concise overview of ownership, delivery, revisions, retention, third-party services, and outcome expectations.

These policies apply to TowleVision production services unless a written project quote states otherwise.

Ask a Question Back to Store

Customer Materials

Ownership and permission

Customers must own or have permission to use submitted videos, scripts, audio, music, images, logos, names, and other materials provided for a project.

Third-party services

Files may pass through upload, payment, email, or cloud-storage services used to receive and deliver the project.

Delivery and Revisions

Delivery

Delivery is usually provided through a private download link. Customers should download and back up delivered files promptly.

Revision pass

One light revision pass is included when stated in the service or quote. The Shorts Pack revision window is 7 days after delivery.

File Retention and AI Training

File retention

TowleVision deletes source, working, and delivery files from active storage after the stated revision window.

AI training

TowleVision does not use customer videos or project files to train AI models.

Outcome Limits

No performance guarantee

TowleVision does not guarantee views, viral performance, platform acceptance, or business outcomes.
