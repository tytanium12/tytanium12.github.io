What the service focuses on

Readable burned-in captions

Captions are formatted to be visible and useful in the final video, with attention to line length, pacing, and contrast.

Caption spelling cleanup

Captions can be cleaned for names, common spelling issues, punctuation, and presentation consistency.

Format-aware output

The final video can be prepared for vertical social use or widescreen viewing when that format fits the project.

Quote-based scope

Pricing depends on video length, caption condition, format needs, and any required cleanup notes.

Start with a short description

You do not need a complete captioning brief to begin. Share what kind of video you have and what you hope to create. Video length, output format, source files, and any words, names, or terms needing special attention can be discussed after the initial review.
