Music-to-Video Production

Create visual videos for rights-cleared music, public-domain works, performances, and experimental music projects.

Good fit

  • Public-domain music projects
  • Original music by the customer
  • Rights-cleared recordings
  • Educational music presentations
  • Experimental visual music pieces
  • Promotional music visuals

What You Send

Music and rights

  • Audio file or accessible link
  • Proof or statement that you own or have permission to use the music
  • Artist or project name
  • Optional logo, social handle, or website

Creative direction

  • Desired tone or style
  • Visual references or themes
  • Preferred format if known
  • Any text or title support needed

What TowleVision Can Provide

Visual concept

A visual direction can be shaped around the track, audience, and intended use.

Image/video sequence

The finished video can use themed imagery or assembled visuals that support the music.

Timing and pacing

The video can be paced around the music so the visuals feel intentionally connected.

Format support

Vertical or widescreen output can be discussed depending on project fit.

Not Included / Limits

  • No copyrighted music without permission.
  • No guarantee of platform monetization or copyright acceptance.
  • No full label or marketing campaign unless separately quoted.
