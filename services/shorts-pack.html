How it works

1. Submit your video

Provide one finished video, confirm you own or have permission to use it, and share the audience, tone, project name, and any useful notes.

2. TowleVision prepares the clips

Strong moments are selected, formatted vertically, captioned for readability, and paired with practical metadata for posting.

3. You receive the package

Your delivery package includes five captioned vertical MP4 clips, a metadata sheet with suggested titles/descriptions/hashtags, and delivery notes.

Revision policy

One light revision pass is included. A light revision may include caption typo corrections, title/description/hashtag tweaks, or replacing one clip if a selected moment is clearly unsuitable. Large re-edits, new source videos, custom motion graphics, or complete clip reselection may require a new order or custom quote.

FAQ

Can I send more than one video?

This package covers one finished source video. Multiple videos can be handled as separate orders or quoted separately.

Do you post the clips for me?

No. The package provides final clips and suggested posting metadata; account management and posting are not included.

What if I need custom graphics?

Custom motion graphics and full brand design are outside the fixed package and may require a custom quote.

What makes a good source video?

Clear speech, useful standalone moments, and a focused audience make the best short-form source material.
