Source video length

The Shorts Pack is designed for one finished source video up to 15 minutes long. Source videos under 5 minutes may produce shorter clips, usually around 10-30 seconds, because there is less source material to work from. Videos under 2 minutes are not a good fit for this package and will be better suited to a future full-video conversion service.

How to send your video

After starting your order, you will provide a download/view link to your finished source video. Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer, OneDrive, unlisted YouTube, or Vimeo links are acceptable. Please make sure the link is accessible before submitting.

Revision policy

Included light revision

One light revision pass is included. Customers have 7 days after delivery to request the included light revision pass. A light revision may include caption typo corrections, title/description/hashtag tweaks, or replacing one clip if a selected moment is clearly unsuitable.

File retention and use

After that 7-day revision window, TowleVision deletes the submitted source video, working files, and delivery files from active storage. If changes are needed after the revision window has closed, a new project may be required.

TowleVision does not use customer videos or project files to train AI models. Files may pass through the upload, payment, email, or cloud-storage services used to receive and deliver the project.

FAQ

Can I send more than one video?

This package covers one finished source video. Multiple videos can be handled as separate orders or quoted separately.

Do you post the clips for me?

No. The package provides final clips and suggested posting metadata; account management and posting are not included.

What if I need custom graphics?

Custom motion graphics and full brand design are outside the fixed package and may require a custom quote.

What makes a good source video?

Clear speech, useful standalone moments, and a focused audience make the best short-form source material.
