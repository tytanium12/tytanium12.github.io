Built Around Four Integrity Checks

The value of a scientific explainer is not just that it looks finished. It needs careful handling of claims, terminology, captions, and visuals.

Scientific Integrity

Technical claims are edited for clarity without overstating what the supplied source material supports.

Pronunciation Integrity

Scientific names, compounds, acronyms, product names, and specialized terms are deliberately prepared for natural narration.

Caption Integrity

Technical spelling, timing, line breaks, synchronization, and readability are reviewed rather than accepted as raw automation.

Visual Integrity

Visuals are selected or generated to support the meaning of the narration - not merely fill the screen.

A Clear Approval Process From Source Material to Final Video

The process is designed to settle the message before production begins, so the final video can be built around approved narration and technical language.

  1. 1 Submit Source Material The client provides a concise project summary, source material, terminology, and intended audience.
  2. 2 Confirm Fit and Scope TowleVision reviews the project and confirms the standard package or identifies work requiring a custom quote.
  3. 3 Prepare the Narration The source material is shaped into a clear, technically responsible spoken script.
  4. 4 Approve the Script The client reviews terminology, claims, names, pronunciations, and required disclaimers before full production.
  5. 5 Produce the Video TowleVision creates narration, visuals, pacing, captions, and the complete publishing package.
  6. 6 Review the Production The client submits one consolidated set of light post-production corrections.
  7. 7 Receive Final Files TowleVision applies approved corrections and delivers the organized final package.

Once approved, the script becomes the production script.

FAQ

What do I need to provide?

A short description of the subject and intended audience is enough to begin. Relevant source material, approved claims, technical terminology, brand assets, and script-approval details can be discussed after the initial review.

Do you write the script?

The standard package includes light editing and adaptation of client-provided material into a focused 150-300-word narration. Extensive research, source synthesis, or writing from disorganized materials may require a custom quote.

How is scientific accuracy handled?

TowleVision compares the narration with the supplied source material, preserves important technical distinctions, and flags unclear or potentially overstated language. The client provides final scientific, legal, and regulatory approval.

Can specialized terminology be pronounced correctly?

Scientific names, compounds, acronyms, and product terminology are deliberately prepared for narration. Clients may provide pronunciation guidance during script review.

Are captions included?

Yes. The final package includes a captioned video, a clean video, and a separate subtitle file. Technical spelling, synchronization, line breaks, and readability are reviewed.

Can I request changes?

One consolidated script-revision round is included before production, followed by one light post-production revision round. Substantial changes to an approved script may require additional time and cost.

Can I use the finished video commercially?

Yes. The final delivered video, thumbnail, captions, and publishing metadata may be used in the client's website, social media, presentations, trade-show displays, sales materials, and other business communications, subject to the service agreement.

Will TowleVision display my project publicly?

Only with permission. Confidential projects will not be publicly displayed as portfolio examples.

How long does production take?

Typical delivery is approximately five business days after script approval, depending on project complexity, source quality, scheduling, and client response time.

Is this medical, legal, or regulatory review?

No. TowleVision provides technical communication and media production rather than independent scientific certification, legal advice, medical review, or regulatory approval.
