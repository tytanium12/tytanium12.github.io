Technical / Accuracy Differentiator

TowleVision is built for media where readability, terminology, pronunciation, review, and delivery structure matter. The focus is practical quality control, not hype.

Readable captions

Captions are treated as part of the viewer experience, with attention to line breaks, contrast, timing, and clean presentation.

Technical terms and glossaries

Consistent delivery

Deliverables are organized so clips, metadata, notes, and source context are easier to use.

Production workflow

TowleVision connects source material, captions, audio, visuals, review, and delivery into repeatable service workflows.

How it works

1. Send the finished video

Share one completed source video up to 15 minutes long, plus basic context: audience, tone, project name, and any must-use or must-avoid moments.

2. TowleVision prepares the clips

The video is reviewed for strong short-form moments, formatted vertically, captioned clearly, and packaged with suggested posting metadata.

3. You receive a clean ZIP

Your delivery package includes five captioned vertical MP4 clips, a metadata sheet with suggested titles/descriptions/hashtags, and delivery notes.
