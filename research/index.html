Original research from systems built to investigate.

TowleVision Insight Lab publishes original research conducted under the supervision of Tyrell R. Towle, Ph.D., using proprietary advanced research software developed by TowleVision. The work documents experimental campaigns, results, failures, stress tests, and follow-up analyses produced by the research program itself.

Publication standard

Methods, failures, and limits belong in the record.

Insight Lab publications are research reports, not promotional summaries. Where the work supports it, reports include negative findings, failed candidates, stress tests, anonymization boundaries, and the limitations needed to interpret the results responsibly.
