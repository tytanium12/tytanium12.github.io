What Survived 1,156 Experiments

An anonymized, regime-aware historical study across 12 established crypto assets, including Bitcoin and Ethereum. The report follows a completed autonomous research campaign through passive benchmarks, cost stress, tail dependence, chronology, and negative, neutral, and positive market conditions.

  • 1,156 experiments screened across 32 research sessions.
  • 31 candidates selected for deeper event-level study.
  • Candidate identities remain anonymized as fixed C01-C12 codes.
  • Historical exploratory research only; no claim of validated future profitability.
