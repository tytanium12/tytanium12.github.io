What the report examines

From broad screening to harder questions

The research campaign did not simply rank backtests by headline return. Strong-looking candidates were challenged with modeled costs, passive buy-and-hold comparisons, drawdown, market exposure, chronological stability, top-winner deletion, and separate negative, neutral, and positive market-regime analysis.

  • How uncommon were strong outcomes across the full 1,156-experiment campaign?
  • Which candidates beat passive holding over the same historical windows?
  • Which results depended on a handful of extreme winners?
  • Which effects persisted when the broad crypto market was flat or declining?
  • Which initially attractive candidates failed under deeper stress testing?
The report is intentionally anonymized. Fixed asset codes C01-C12 preserve the internal scientific relationships while withholding the identity key.
